The Hidden Dangers of a Closed Property in Cairo

Whether you have been overseas for months or are leasing a villa that has been sitting empty, a closed property in Cairo deteriorates rapidly. The combination of intense heat, lack of ventilation, and fine desert dust creates a perfect breeding ground for biological hazards.

Stagnant plumbing breeds bacteria, dormant AC ducts accumulate thick layers of allergens, and closed bathrooms often develop hidden mold and mildew. A traditional surface clean simply pushes these hazards around. Your home requires a complete biological reset.
