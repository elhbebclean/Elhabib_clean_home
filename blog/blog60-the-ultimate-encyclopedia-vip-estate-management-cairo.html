The Turnkey Post-Construction Handover

The transition from a chaotic construction site to a pristine, move-in-ready sanctuary is the most critical phase in a property's lifecycle. A multi-million-pound renovation can be instantly ruined by standard cleaners who use corrosive acids to remove cement or wet mops that turn silica dust into concrete-like sludge in your floor joints.

Habib Clean’s Turnkey Handover protocol synchronizes multiple engineering divisions. We deploy industrial HEPA vacuums for dry dust extraction, preventing dust from entering your central HVAC systems. Simultaneously, our stone division executes diamond grinding to safely remove contractor debris from your marble, while our facade team details architectural glass without causing micro-scratches.

  • Zero-Acid cement removal protocols.
  • Dry silica dust extraction from suspended ceilings and ductwork.
  • Safe paint and silicone removal from panoramic windows and aluminum profiles.
02

The Art of Marble Grinding & Crystallization

Natural stone, particularly imported Italian Statuario, Calacatta, and Spanish Emperador, is the crown jewel of any luxury estate. Over time, heavy foot traffic, improper acidic cleaners, and shifting foundations cause scratches, dullness, and 'lippage' (uneven tiles).

Our Marble Mastery division employs heavy-duty planetary grinders fitted with progressive diamond resin pads. We physically shave off the damaged micro-layer of the stone, perfectly leveling the floor. We then utilize European crystallization compounds that react chemically with the calcium in the stone, creating a breathtaking, mirror-like finish that is highly resistant to future scuffs.

  • Lippage removal for a seamless, grout-free appearance.
  • Epoxy color-matching for crack and fissure repairs.
  • Chemical-free mechanical buffing for enduring brilliance.

Exterior Facade & High-Altitude Restoration

The exterior of your villa is constantly battling Cairo's harsh desert environment. Sun-baked sand, urban pollution, and acid rain degrade both glass and natural stone over time. Habib Clean deploys advanced Deionized (DI) Water-Fed Pole Systems to clean high-altitude panoramic windows safely from the ground. This pure water acts as a magnet for dirt and dries completely streak-free.

For Hashimi and Pharaonic stone boundary walls, we use calibrated high-pressure washing combined with eco-friendly treatments to strip away calcification and smog, restoring the vibrant original color of the stone without damaging your landscape or flower beds.

VIP Interior Detailing & Built-In Kitchens

Cleaning is about removing visible dirt; detailing is about restoring a space to a 5-star standard. Our Platinum Detailing protocol focuses on the microscopic elements of your home. We manually polish woodwork with European waxes, shine stainless steel fixtures, and extract dust from hidden tracks.

In high-end kitchens, we strictly prohibit the use of harsh degreasers that strip the finish off acrylic cabinets and natural marble countertops. Instead, we use dry steam vapor (up to 160°C) to safely melt carbon from built-in ovens and biologically sanitize refrigerators, ensuring a 100% food-safe environment.

05

On-Site Fine Textile & Parquet Preservation

Sending a $10,000 Persian rug to a commercial laundry is a massive risk. Colors bleed, wool shrinks, and assets get lost. We eliminated this risk by bringing advanced low-moisture dry extraction technology directly into your villa. Your silk and wool carpets are cleaned, sanitized, and dried on-site without ever leaving your sight.

Similarly, for expensive Parquet and HDF floors, water is the ultimate enemy, causing warping and rot. We employ a strict waterless protocol, using mechanical buffing and solid waxes to nourish the wood and create a protective, water-repellent seal.

06

Biological Safety: Water Tanks & Closed Villas

For expats arriving in Cairo, health and safety are paramount. Stagnant water in rooftop tanks breeds Legionella, rust, and algae. Before you move in, our technicians drain, scrub, and biologically disinfect your entire water storage system using food-safe sanitizers, followed by a high-pressure plumbing flush.

If you are returning to a villa that has been closed for months, you face stagnant air, hidden mold in AC vents, and massive dust accumulation. Our Biological Sanitization protocol uses ozone treatments, HEPA vacuums, and medical-grade disinfectants to execute a complete environmental reset of the property.
