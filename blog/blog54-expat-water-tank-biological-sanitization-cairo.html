The Hidden Hazard: Cairo's Stored Water Systems

When relocating to Egypt, many international residents are unaware that almost all luxury villas and apartment buildings rely on rooftop or underground water tanks for their daily supply. While the municipal water is treated, the storage tanks themselves often sit unmaintained for years.

Fine desert dust penetrates these tanks, settling at the bottom to form a thick sludge. Combined with Cairo's intense summer heat, this stagnant environment becomes a breeding ground for green algae, rust from degrading pipes, and dangerous waterborne bacteria like Legionella. Showering in or brushing your teeth with this water poses a severe health risk to your family.
