The Hidden Danger in Your Luxury Kitchen

The kitchen is the heart of your home, but it is also a magnet for vaporized cooking oils, hidden food spills, and bacteria. When expats hire standard domestic helpers or commercial cleaners to tackle a greasy kitchen, a catastrophic mistake often occurs: the misuse of harsh, acidic degreasers.

Cheap degreasing chemicals will instantly dull the high-gloss lacquer of imported acrylic cabinets, strip the protective sealant off expensive natural marble countertops, and leave toxic chemical residues inside ovens and microwaves where your food is prepared.
