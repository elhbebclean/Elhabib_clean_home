The Hidden Danger: Why Wet Mopping Ruins Wood

Many expat homes and classic apartments in Cairo feature breathtaking natural parquet or high-end HDF flooring. However, a common and costly mistake made by untrained domestic staff is using traditional wet mops and chemical detergents to clean these surfaces.

Wood is a living, porous material. When exposed to excess water, the moisture seeps into the seams, causing the wood to expand, warp (cupping), and eventually rot. The finish becomes dull, and the floor loses its luxurious appeal.

The Elhabib Clean Waterless Protocol

At Elhabib Clean, we treat hardwood floors with the reverence they deserve. Our specialized process ensures zero water damage and maximum shine:

  • Dry Debris Extraction: Utilizing high-suction industrial vacuums with soft-bristle attachments to safely remove micro-dust and grit that cause surface scratches.
