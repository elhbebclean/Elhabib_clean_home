Phase 1: Diamond Marble Grinding & Polishing

Our stone restoration division steps in first. We do not use acids. We use heavy-duty planetary grinders equipped with industrial diamond resin pads to gently shave off contractor debris, cement haze, and level any uneven tiles (lippage). This is followed by an Italian crystallization process that gives your brand-new marble a flawless, mirror-like finish.

Phase 2: Architectural Glass & Facade Detailing

While the floors are being restored, our high-altitude and glass divisions tackle your windows and facades. Using medical-grade scraper blades and eco-friendly solvents, we safely lift silicone, masking tape residue, and paint overspray from your glass without leaving microscopic scratches, restoring 100% optical clarity.

Phase 3: Silica Dust Extraction & Final Sterilization

Fine construction dust (silica) hides inside AC ducts, suspended ceilings, and electrical tracks. If wet-mopped, it turns into a stubborn cement film. We deploy industrial 3-motor HEPA vacuums to extract this dust completely dry. Finally, we sanitize all bathrooms and kitchens, leaving the estate clinically sterile and move-in ready.
