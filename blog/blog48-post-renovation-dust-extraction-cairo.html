Navigating the Aftermath of Renovations in Cairo

Renovating a villa or apartment is a massive investment. However, the joy of a newly finished home is often overshadowed by the reality of post-construction mess. Contractors in Egypt frequently leave behind a hazardous mix of fine silica dust, cement splatters on new Italian marble, and paint overspray on expensive aluminum window frames.

Hiring standard cleaners for this stage is the biggest mistake property owners make. Unskilled labor relies on heavy water usage and highly corrosive acids (like muriatic acid) which permanently etch new marble, warp fresh wooden doors, and rust luxury bathroom fixtures.

Our Scientific Post-Construction Protocol

At Elhabib Clean, we approach post-renovation cleanup as a delicate restorative science:

  • Dry Silica Extraction: Before a single drop of water is used, we deploy 3-motor industrial vacuums to extract fine dust from AC vents, suspended ceilings, and hidden tracks. If wet mopped, this dust turns into a stubborn, impossible-to-remove cement film.
  • Acid-Free Cement Removal: We use safe, eco-friendly solvents and surgical-grade scrapers to gently detach cement and grout haze from porcelain and marble without scratching the glaze.
  • Window & Frame Detailing: Removing paint, silicone, and masking tape residue from architectural glass and aluminum frames without leaving microscopic scratches.
  • Final Sterilization: Once the structural debris is cleared, we execute a full VIP deep clean, sanitizing all bathrooms and kitchens so your family can move in safely the very next day.
