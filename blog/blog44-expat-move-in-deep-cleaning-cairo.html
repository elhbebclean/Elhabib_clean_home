Our Comprehensive Transition Protocol

We don't just clean surfaces; we detail every corner of your property to ensure a safe, healthy environment for your family:

  • Kitchen Degreasing: Total sterilization of cabinets (inside and out), extractor hoods, and appliances to remove all traces of previous occupants.
  • Bathroom Sterilization: Acid-wash of grout lines, descaling of shower glasses, and biological disinfection of all sanitary ware.

  • Air Quality Control: Industrial vacuuming of central AC vents to remove trapped construction dust and allergens.
