Core Services for International Arrivals & Expats

1. 100% On-Site Rug & Carpet Care (Zero Removal)

Don't risk your expensive Persian, Turkish, or silk carpets being hauled away to external washing plants where colors bleed or fibers warp. Our specialists perform advanced low-moisture dry extraction and steam sanitization directly inside your home. Your carpets are cleaned, deodorized, and ready to walk on within hours.

2. Water Tank Biological Disinfection

Water safety is the number one concern for visitors and expats relocating to Egypt. Before you drink, shower, or cook, our technicians drain, scrub, and biologically sanitize your rooftop or underground water tanks, ensuring crystal-clear, germ-free water flowing through your taps from day one.

3. Parquet Polishing & Hardwood Restoration

Classic apartments in Zamalek and luxury villas in New Cairo feature magnificent wooden floors. We apply waterless European wax buffing to nourish dried-out wood and restore a brilliant, protective luster without risking water damage.
