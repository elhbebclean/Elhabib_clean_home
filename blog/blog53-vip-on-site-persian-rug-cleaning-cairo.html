The Million-Pound Mistake: Traditional Rug Laundries

Your authentic Persian, Turkish, or handmade silk rug is more than just decor; it is an appreciating asset often worth thousands of dollars. The greatest threat to these masterpieces is not foot traffic—it is traditional cleaning companies.

When you allow a company to roll up your high-value rug and load it onto a truck, you surrender control. Off-site commercial laundries routinely submerge delicate silk and wool in massive vats of water mixed with cheap industrial detergents. The result? Irreversible color bleeding, structural shrinkage, loss of natural sheen, and in worst-case scenarios, the misplacement of your valuable asset.

The Habib Clean "Zero Removal" Protocol

We engineered our services exclusively for elite properties. We do not take your rugs away; we bring the pinnacle of textile restoration technology directly into your home, executing the process safely over your marble or parquet floors without a single drop of water seeping through.
