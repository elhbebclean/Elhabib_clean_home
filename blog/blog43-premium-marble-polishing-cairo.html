The Art of Stone Restoration

Marble is a living stone. Over time, high traffic, acidic spills, and improper cleaning methods strip away its natural luster, leaving behind a dull, scratched surface. Standard cleaning companies often apply temporary chemical waxes to hide these imperfections—a practice that ultimately yellows and suffocates the stone.

At Elhabib Clean, we don't just clean; we restore the stone structurally.

Advanced Grinding Techniques

Our 5-Step Diamond Grinding Protocol

Our expat and VIP clients demand perfection. That is why we rely on a mechanical, chemical-free grinding process using graded diamond discs:

  • Lippage Removal: Flattening the surface to eliminate uneven edges between tiles.
  • Honing: Removing deep scratches, stains, and acid etching from the top layer.
  • Polishing: Using ultra-fine diamond pads to bring out the stone's inherent shine.
  • Crystallization: A thermochemical reaction that hardens the surface, making it mirror-like and highly resistant to future scuffs.
