The Art of Stone Restoration

Marble is a living stone. Over time, high traffic, acidic spills, and improper cleaning methods strip away its natural luster, leaving behind a dull, scratched surface. Standard cleaning companies often apply temporary chemical waxes to hide these imperfections—a practice that ultimately yellows and suffocates the stone.
