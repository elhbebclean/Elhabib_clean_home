The Challenge of Cairo's Desert Climate

Owning a standalone villa in Cairo comes with unique maintenance challenges. Seasonal sandstorms (Khamaseen), intense UV exposure, and urban smog quickly degrade the exterior aesthetics of luxury estates. Panoramic glass windows become opaque with micro-dust, while expensive natural stone fences (Hashimi and Pharaonic stone) accumulate dirt, calcification, and bird droppings, causing them to look aged and weathered.

Domestic staff simply do not have the equipment or expertise to maintain high-reach facades or restore natural stone. Attempting to wash these surfaces with standard hoses often leads to water pooling, mud creation, and permanent hard-water stains on your glass.

High-End Execution Standards

Phase 1: Streak-Free Panoramic Glass Restoration

We do not use standard squeegees or risky ladders that can crush your expensive landscaping. Our facade division uses Deionized (DI) Water-Fed Pole Systems. This technology allows our technicians to clean high-altitude glass safely from the ground. The pure, mineral-free water effortlessly cuts through desert dust and dries naturally without leaving a single streak or hard-water spot.

Phase 2: Hashimi Stone & Boundary Wall Revival

The boundary walls of your estate are the first thing guests see. We utilize calibrated high-pressure washing combined with eco-friendly stone treatments to strip away embedded dirt, smog, and algae from natural Hashimi stone, granite, and interlocking driveway tiles. This chemical-free process reveals the vibrant, original color of the stone without eroding its structural integrity.

Phase 3: Outdoor Living Spaces & Patios

A luxury villa's outdoor patio and poolside area should be an immaculate extension of your indoor living space. We deep clean outdoor pergolas, polish exterior marble decks, and sanitize barbecue areas, ensuring your estate is perfectly primed for hosting guests and enjoying the Egyptian evenings.
