The #1 Enemy of Your Expensive Hardwood Floors

Many expats moving into beautiful historic apartments or newly built villas inherit breathtaking natural parquet or imported HDF flooring. However, a common and catastrophic mistake made by untrained domestic staff in Egypt is using wet mops and generic detergents to clean these surfaces.

Wood is a highly porous, living material. When exposed to standing water or damp mops, moisture inevitably seeps into the micro-seams between the panels. Within months, this causes the wood to swell, cup (warp), and eventually rot from the inside out. Once warped, the only solution is a complete, costly replacement.

The Elhabib Clean Waterless Solution

At Elhabib Clean, we treat high-end wooden floors as an architectural investment. Our specialized process ensures zero water damage while maximizing the wood's natural glow:

  • Dry Micro-Extraction: We utilize industrial vacuums equipped with ultra-soft horsehair brush attachments to safely lift micro-dust and grit from the grain without scratching the surface.
  • Evaporative Spot Treatment: Sticky residues and scuff marks are treated individually using pH-neutral, wood-safe solvents that evaporate instantly, leaving no moisture behind.
  • Mechanical Wax Buffing: We apply premium European solid waxes, using professional low-speed rotary machines equipped with soft buffing pads. This friction melts the wax deep into the wood's pores, nourishing dried-out fibers and creating a brilliant, slip-resistant, water-repellent seal.
