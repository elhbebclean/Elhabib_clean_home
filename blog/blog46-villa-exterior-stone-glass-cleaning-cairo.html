Our Dual-Action Exterior Protocol

1. Hashimi & Natural Stone Restoration

Natural stones like Hashimi, Pharaonic, and Mica are stunning but highly porous. We utilize controlled hydro-blasting (pressure washing) calibrated specifically for natural stone. This process safely extracts years of accumulated dust, vehicle exhaust, and biological growth (algae) without eroding the delicate surface texture of the stone.

2. High-Altitude Architectural Glass Cleaning

Villas with grand entrance windows or massive glass facades require specialized access. Elhabib Clean employs certified technicians utilizing professional scaffolding and rope access techniques. We treat your glass panels with eco-friendly, anti-static solutions that not only provide a streak-free crystalline finish but also repel future dust accumulation.
