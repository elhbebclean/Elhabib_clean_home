Operational FAQ

Can you manage my Cairo property if I live abroad?

Yes. Our property management infrastructure is expressly designed for overseas property owners, expats, and international investors. We act as your local operational partner in Egypt, handling guest communication, maintenance, and check-ins transparently.

Do you provide turnover cleaning for Airbnb properties?

Yes. All turnover and deep cleaning services are executed exclusively by our dedicated in-house division, Elhabib Clean. We do not outsource to unverified freelancers, ensuring consistent hygiene standards.

What areas of Cairo do you cover?

We currently provide on-ground property management operations in Maadi, Katameya, Zahraa El Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and the Fifth Settlement (Tagamoa).
